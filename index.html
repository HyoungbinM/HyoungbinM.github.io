About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Social & Cultural Psychology Lab at Yonsei University, advised by Young-Hoon Kim. My research examines how romantic relationships form, change, and endure: what people value in a partner, how perceived mate value shapes satisfaction, and how attachment and social-ecological context influence relationship transitions such as marriage and parenthood.

Methodologically, I work with dyadic longitudinal data and specialize in the Actor–Partner Interdependence Model, dyadic response surface analysis, multilevel and longitudinal modeling, and machine-learning approaches to forecasting relationship outcomes.
